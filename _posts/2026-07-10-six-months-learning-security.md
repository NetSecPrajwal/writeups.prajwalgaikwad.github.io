Six months ago I started taking cybersecurity seriously — not just as an interest, but as a direction. Here's what that's actually been like.

## It's slower than the content suggests

YouTube tutorials make it look fast. Someone spins up a box, pops a shell in 12 minutes, explains the whole attack path cleanly. In reality I've spent three hours on boxes that took them 12 minutes. I've googled the same `curl` flags four times in a week.

The gap between "watching someone do it" and "doing it yourself" is enormous. That gap is where the learning actually happens.

## Imposter syndrome is loud early on

There's a moment — usually around week 3 — where you realize how much you don't know. The field is massive. Web security alone could take years to fully understand. Then there's network security, reverse engineering, malware analysis, cloud security, red teaming...

It's easy to feel like you're not progressing because you keep discovering new things you don't know.

What helped: picking one area (web app pentesting), going deep on it, and accepting that breadth would come later.

## What's actually moved the needle

**PortSwigger Web Security Academy.** Free, structured, hands-on. I've learned more from those labs than anything else.

**Writing things down.** This blog is partly that. When I have to explain something clearly enough to write it, I realize where my understanding is fuzzy.

**Accepting that boxes are supposed to be hard.** If I'm not stuck, I'm probably not learning anything new.

**Talking to other learners.** Discord communities, CTF teams, people who are six months ahead of where I am. The field is more collaborative than competitive.

## Where I am now

Two certifications (CNSP, CSEDP — both with Merit), one more (WEB-RTA), actively doing labs, building tools, and writing here.

I'm not where I want to be yet. But six months ago I couldn't read a Nmap scan confidently. Progress is real, even when it's not visible.

---

If you're also early in this journey and it feels slow — it's not just you. Keep going.
