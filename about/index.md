  <h2>Hey there</h2>
  <p>
    I'm Prajwal — a cybersecurity student from Pune, Maharashtra with a deep interest in offensive security, penetration testing, and web application security. During the day I work as a Network Engineer. After hours I'm usually rooting boxes, hunting bugs, or writing about what I'm learning.
  </p>
  <p>
    This blog is where I document things that feel worth remembering — CTF writeups (the full process, not just the flag), security research notes, and occasionally just personal thoughts on the industry and life in it.
  </p>

  <h2>What I work with</h2>
  <p>
    Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, OWASP ZAP, Python, Bash, PowerShell — the usual toolkit. Certified in CNSP and CSEDP (both with Merit) from The SecOps Group, WEB-RTA from Cyberwarfare Labs, and working my way toward eJPT → PNPT → OSCP.
  </p>

  <h2>Why I write</h2>
  <p>
    Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain something clearly enough to write it down, I probably don't understand it as well as I thought. So the writeups here are as much for me as they are for anyone reading.
  </p>
  <p>
    If something here helps you get a flag, understand a concept, or just gives you a different angle on a problem — that's enough.
  </p>
